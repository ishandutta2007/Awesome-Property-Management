# Awesome-Property-Management

## Top Property Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Residential & Multifamily Property Management, Tenant Portals, Leasing, Maintenance, Accounting & Owner Reporting*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Property Management**. These systems help landlords and property managers handle units, tenants, leases, rent collection, maintenance work orders, accounting, and owner reporting across single-family, multifamily, and mixed portfolios.



**Examples** include AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, DoorLoop, Rent Manager, Propertyware, Hemlane, Entrata, Folio, and TurboTenant (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured property management platforms remain almost entirely commercial. Open options exist but are limited in maturity, integrations, and feature depth compared with leading SaaS products. This section lists the strongest available projects and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/)**  

  Feature-rich, AI-enhanced property management platform popular with professional managers for automation, leasing, accounting, and operations.



- **[Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/)**  

  Comprehensive property management platform with strong accounting, leasing, tenant portals, and scalability for growing portfolios.



- **[Yardi](https://www.yardi.com/)**  

  Enterprise-grade property management suite widely used for large multifamily, commercial, and complex real-estate portfolios.



- **[DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/)**  

  Modern all-in-one property management platform known for ease of use, transparent pricing, and solid mid-market capabilities.



- **[Rent Manager](https://www.rentmanager.com/)**  

  Flexible property management software supporting residential and commercial portfolios with strong accounting and customization options.



- **[Propertyware](https://www.propertyware.com/)**  

  Property management platform especially strong for single-family and scattered-site residential portfolios.



- **[Hemlane](https://www.hemlane.com/)**  

  Property management platform that combines software with optional full-service management support for residential landlords.



- **[Entrata](https://www.entrata.com/)**  

  Multifamily property management and resident experience platform used by larger apartment operators.



- **[Folio](https://www.folio.co/)** (or similar modern platforms)  

  Cloud property management solutions focused on streamlined operations and owner/tenant experiences.



- **[TurboTenant](https://www.turbotenant.com/)**  

  Landlord-focused platform offering free and paid tools for listings, screening, rent collection, and basic management.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Evaluating self-hosted open property managers when data ownership and customization are top priorities.

- Using Odoo or ERPNext as a foundation for more complex multi-entity or accounting needs.

- Combining open lease/tenant trackers with separate payment processors, screening services, and accounting tools for small portfolios.

- Accepting that mature tenant screening integrations, automated rent collection at scale, polished mobile apps, advanced owner portals, and compliance features still favor commercial platforms.

- Starting with free or low-cost commercial tools (TurboTenant and similar) before investing heavily in open-source customization.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Self-host an open property manager → track units, tenants, and leases → handle rent and basic maintenance → integrate external payments and screening → produce owner reports via exports or simple dashboards. This approach can work for smaller portfolios with technical capacity. Commercial platforms (AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, DoorLoop, Rent Manager, Propertyware, Entrata, etc.) remain the practical choice for most professional managers who need reliability, compliance features, and support without heavy custom development.
